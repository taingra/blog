Welcome to my personal website.  I work in technology and I'm
primarily interested in the intersection of business, communication,
data, and tech.  I've worked at Comerica Bank for 5+ years.  In my
role I support our Credit applications and their on-going development.

In my free time I enjoy gardening, drawing, and writing.  I am a
volunteer [[https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/free-sw.en.html][Free Software]] developer interested in [[https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/][GNU Emacs]] and the
GNU+Linux ecosystem.  I primarily use this site to blog about
technology, but my other interests may appear here as well.  To
receive updates when new blog posts are published please subscribe to
my low-volume [[file:rss.org][RSS feed]].
