Remy and I walked the full red loop, so around 5 miles.  Pretty nice
trail that goes by some small ponds and has some nice hills.

Low rating because of the close proximity to the shooting range.  On a
Saturday at around 2 PM the gunfire was basically constant the entire
walk and very loud for about 2 miles of the trail.  Your experience
may vary.
